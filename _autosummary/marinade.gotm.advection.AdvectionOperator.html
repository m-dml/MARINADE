AdvectionOperator¶

class marinade.gotm.advection.AdvectionOperator(bc_up_type: str, bc_down_type: str, bc_up_value: torch.Tensor | float | int, bc_down_value: torch.Tensor | float | int, scheme: str, conserve: bool, *args, **kwargs)[source]¶

Advection operator class.

Should be initialized with the important numerical parameters and schemes for the simulation. Has a forward method for computing a single time step. Calling the operator object itself also calls its forward method.

The operator solves the conservative or non-conservative advection equation:

\[\begin{split}\frac{\partial Y}{\partial t} &= -\frac{\partial F}{\partial z} \qquad && \text{(Conservative form)} \\ \frac{\partial Y}{\partial t} &= -\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial z} - Y \frac{\partial w}{\partial z}\right) \qquad && \text{(Non-conservative form)}\end{split}\]

\(F\) is the flux field due to the velocity \(w\). It is computed in an upstream-biased way:

\[F_i^n = \frac{1}{\Delta t}\int_{z_i^{\text{Face}} - w \Delta t}^{z_i^{\text{Face}}} Y(z') \,\text{dz'}\]
Parameters:

  • bc_up_type (str) – Type of upper boundary condition (“flux”, “value”, “oneSided”, “zeroDivergence”).

  • bc_down_type (str) – Type of lower boundary condition (“flux”, “value”, “oneSided”, “zeroDivergence”).

  • bc_up_value (Union[torch.Tensor, float, int]) – Value for upper boundary condition. Scalar or [batch, tracer]

  • bc_down_value (Union[torch.Tensor, float, int]) – Value for lower boundary condition. Scalar or [batch, tracer]

  • scheme (str) – Advection limiter scheme to use (“superbee”, “p2_pdm”, “splmax13”, “muscl”, “central”, “p2”).

  • conserve (bool) – Whether to use the conservative form of the advection equation.

Parameters:

  • concentration (Union[torch.Tensor, TensorDict]) – The initial tracer concentration field. Can be a TensorDict. Then this operator acts on concentration[“pelagic”]. This functionality exists because benthic (sediment) variables might be part of the tracer state, but they are only on the ground and do not advect. [batch, tracer, nlev]

  • dt (float) – The time step.

  • h (torch.Tensor) – The layer heights. [batch, 1, nlev]

  • w (torch.Tensor) –

    The velocity field. This can be:

    • [batch, (tracer or 1), nlev]: Values at cell centers

    • [batch, (tracer or 1), nlev+1]: Values at cell faces.

    • [batch, (tracer or 1), 1]: Constant velocity field (also at domain boundaries).
